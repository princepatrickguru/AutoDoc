Health support at a glance

Understand what each risk level means and the next steps to consider.

Green

Low risk

Symptoms may be suitable for home care and monitoring. Follow appropriate self-care guidance and seek medical advice if symptoms persist or worsen.

Yellow

Caution

Medical review may be needed soon. Arrange an appropriate healthcare assessment, especially if symptoms are worsening or new warning signs appear.

Red

Emergency

Possible medical emergency. Seek emergency medical care immediately. Do not delay urgent treatment while waiting for online guidance.

These color levels provide general guidance, not a diagnosis. If you believe a situation is urgent, seek medical care regardless of the assigned color.
